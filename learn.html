Learn · Patient correspondence

Recall & reminder letters

Three judgment skills, one real task — about four minutes, then you'll prove it in AIGround.

01

Give the AI context

The AI can't sound like your clinic unless you tell it who you are, the tone you want, and how patients book. Vague in, generic out — so name the clinic, the feeling ("warm, plain-English"), and the call to action.

✓ Do

"Warm, plain-English recall letter for Riverstone Family Practice. Invite them to book online or by phone."

✕ Don't

"Write a recall letter."

02

Keep unsafe data out

Names, dates of birth, Medicare numbers and clinical results are sensitive health information. A general AI tool never needs them to draft a letter — and pasting them in can be an unauthorised disclosure. Describe the situation, not the person.

✓ Do

"a patient overdue for a routine review"

✕ Don't

"Margaret Nguyen, DOB 14/03/1958, Medicare 2953 8881 71, borderline high result"

03

Verify what it gives back

AI writes confidently even when it's wrong — it will invent a phone number or a date to fill a gap. Treat every draft as a first version: leave placeholders for anything you can't confirm, and check facts against the real source before it goes out.

✓ Do

Leave "[booking phone]" and confirm it before sending.

✕ Don't

Trust an invented number: "call us on (03) 9412 6680".
